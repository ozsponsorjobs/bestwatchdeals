💬 Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I wind a mechanical chronograph?

Wind the crown clockwise 20-25 turns each morning until you feel gentle resistance. Do not force past resistance.

Can the chronograph function run continuously?

It is best practice to run the chronograph only when timing events to preserve the mainspring power reserve and minimize gear wear.
